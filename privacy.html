PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: October 22, 2026

1. Who we are

INFINITY BLACK ("we", "us") is an independent music channel producing original phonk and funk content, operating the YouTube channel https://www.youtube.com/@infinityblack789. This website and our developer app are operated solely by us.

2. What we collect

When the owner of our TikTok account connects our app, TikTok shares with us:

  • your TikTok open ID — a pseudonymous identifier that does not reveal your identity;
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, used only to call TikTok's Content Posting API;
  • basic profile information returned by the approved scopes (such as display name and avatar).

We do not collect any other personal data from anyone. The app acts only on our own account.

3. How we use this data

  • to upload and publish videos produced by us to our own TikTok account;
  • to check the posting status of those videos;
  • to keep the connection between our app and our TikTok account working (token refresh).

We never post on behalf of any other user and we never access third-party data.

4. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not use the data for advertising or tracking.
  • We do not collect data from the people who watch our videos through this app.

5. Storage and security

Tokens and identifiers are stored securely with restricted access, and are used only for the purposes described above. We apply technical measures to keep this data private.

6. Retention and revocation

We keep the connection data only while the TikTok connection is active. You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok settings (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions) or by contacting us. After revocation, we delete all stored tokens and identifiers.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page with a new "last updated" date.

8. Contact

Questions? Contact us at @infinityblack789 (YouTube or TikTok).
